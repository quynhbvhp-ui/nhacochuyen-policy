# Privacy Policy — Nhà Có Chuyện Uploader

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

## Data the app accesses

- The TikTok access token and refresh token of the owner's own account, obtained through TikTok
  Login Kit with the `video.upload` scope only.

## How it is stored

Tokens are stored in a single file on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's user
account. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other TikTok user.
- It does not read comments, followers, messages or analytics.
- It does not publish videos; it only places them in the owner's drafts inbox.

## Deletion

Revoking the app in TikTok settings, or deleting the local token file, removes all access.
