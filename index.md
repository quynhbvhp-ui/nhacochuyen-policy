# Terms of Service — Nhà Có Chuyện Uploader

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

**Nhà Có Chuyện Uploader** ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the TikTok
account *Nhà Có Chuyện*. It is not offered to the public.

## What the app does

The app uploads short fictional story videos, produced by the owner, to the **drafts inbox of the
owner's own TikTok account**. Nothing is published automatically: the owner opens TikTok, reviews
each draft, adds music and caption, and decides whether to post it.

## Who may use it

Only the account owner. No other TikTok users can sign in or upload through the app.

## Content

All videos are original fictional stories, narrated with AI-generated voice and labelled as
AI-generated content when posted, in line with TikTok's Community Guidelines.
